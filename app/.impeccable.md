# Impeccable Design Context — Sumo Trainer

## Design Context

### Users
Curious newcomers to professional sumo — people just discovering the sport who want an approachable, enjoyable way to learn terminology, techniques, and wrestlers. They don't know their yokozuna from their maegashira yet, and the app should feel like a friendly guide rather than an exam. The vibe is a quiet Saturday afternoon learning something new, not a competitive study grind.

### Brand Personality
**Calm. Authentic. Welcoming.**

The app is a dojo, not a classroom. It should feel like stepping into a space that has earned its gravity through simplicity and restraint — not one that's trying to impress you. Cultural depth is present but never gatekept.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Zen / wabi-sabi** — restrained, quiet, imperfect beauty.

The existing palette is already well-suited:
- `rice` (#f8f1de) — warm cream, like washi paper
- `ink` (#1b1a17) — deep brown-black, like sumi ink
- `navy` (#27386e) — like ai-zome (indigo-dyed fabric)
- `clay` (#b55233) — like terracotta roof tile
- `pine` (#1f5c4d) — like moss on stone
- `gold` (#d4a017) — like gilded lacquerware

Japanese flair should be expressed through **sparse kanji/hiragana as typographic texture** — meaningful labels, subtle dividers, or motifs — not decoration for its own sake. Think a single 相撲 as a header accent, or 始める alongside "Start". Never tourist-trap ornament.

No gradients, no heavy shadows, no glossy effects. Components should feel matte and natural — ink on paper, not plastic.

**Anti-references**: No cherry-blossom clipart, no red-sun clichés, no lantern/sushi imagery. No dark mode. No loud animations.

### Design Principles

1. **Breathing room is the design** — whitespace is intentional, not leftover. Generous margins and padding signal craft.
2. **Ink over plastic** — matte surfaces, subtle borders, no gloss. Components should feel like they were drawn, not extruded.
3. **One Japanese element that earns its place** — sparse kanji/hiragana used structurally (as labels, dividers, or header accents) rather than scattered as decoration.
4. **Approachable depth** — cultural richness revealed progressively. The first screen should feel welcoming; complexity comes later as the user earns it.
5. **Restraint as craft** — the temptation to add is always wrong. Remove until the design can't be simplified further without losing meaning.
